I am currently a master's student in the Degree Program in Intelligent and Mechanical Interaction Systems (IMIS) at the University of Tsukuba. Before that, I received my Bachelor of Engineering degree in Software Engineering from Northeastern University in 2024. My research interests focus on computer vision, 3D reconstruction, human motion understanding, sports video analysis, and vision-language models (VLMs).

I enjoy building AI systems that connect research ideas with real-world applications. My previous work covers 3D-aware vision-language reasoning, LLM-based dialogue systems, and full-stack web development.

# Highlights

- Co-first author of **[VisionCube: 3D-Aware Vision-Language Model for Multi-Step Spatial Reasoning]({{ site.baseurl }}/publications/)**, accepted to a CVPR 2025 Workshop.
- Experience in 3D reconstruction, vision-language models, LLM-based dialogue systems, and retrieval-augmented generation.
- Built an **[e-commerce shopping guide system based on LLM and Retrieval-Augmented Generation (RAG)]({{ site.baseurl }}/projects/)** using Flask, Vue.js, PostgreSQL, and a BERT-based intent recognition module.
- Software engineering internship experience in backend and frontend development using Spring Boot, Spring Cloud, MyBatis, Vue.js, and ElementUI.
